# tipjar+ launches a clear USDC tipping flow for creators

**Lead (≤ 40 words):**

tipjar+ introduces a short, transparent USDC tipping flow designed for creators and their audiences — a few guided taps from intent to confirmation.

## Highlights

tipjar+ provides a straightforward path for fans to appreciate creators. The experience centers on a clear profile with a “tip” button, an amount selection, and an unmistakable confirmation. Instead of multiple screens and distractions, users follow a single, predictable track that reduces friction and errors.

## Why clarity matters

In micro-support, timing is everything. Long or confusing flows cause drop-off. tipjar+ focuses on simplicity: few steps, predictable UI, and no unnecessary fields. Creators gain a consistent way to receive tips without complicating their channels.

## USDC — descriptive context

USDC is a digital unit designed to maintain a stable 1:1 reference to the US dollar (descriptive). In tipping, it provides a simple value reference for both fans and creators. tipjar+ materials avoid financial claims; the emphasis is on user experience and clarity.

## Accessibility and good practice

The project prioritizes readable thumbnails and messages, ALT text ≤ 120 characters, at least AA contrast, and brief confirmations. Captions support viewers who watch without sound. The goal is to make tipping a quick gesture of appreciation, not a task.

## Assets for editors

Our newsroom includes PL/EN press releases, concise key messages, company boilerplate, founder bio, media Q&A, logos, KV, and example screenshots. Each asset includes ALT, and product/help links use UTM to enable basic engagement measurement.

## Availability and contact

All press materials, graphics, and FAQ live in a single newsroom page. The team is available for concise, factual product discussions without any financial performance claims.
