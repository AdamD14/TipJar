## Metryki do śledzenia skuteczności

### Creator-Side KPIs
- **Monthly Active Creators (MAC)**: Target \>5% miesięczny wzrost
- **Creator Revenue Growth**: MoM earnings increase per creator
- **Creator Churn Rate**: \<5% miesięcznie (vs 8-12% industry average)
- **Content Velocity**: Posts/streams per creator per month
- **Revenue Concentration**: Gini coefficient for earnings distribution

### Fan-Side KPIs
- **Monthly Active Supporters (MAS)**: Fans making regular payments
- **Average Revenue Per User (ARPU)**: Target $15-25 monthly spending
- **Tip Conversion Rate**: % content views → micropayments (target 15-25%)
- **Multi-Creator Support**: % fans supporting 3+ creators
- **Fan Lifetime Value (LTV)**: Projected total platform spending

### Cross-Platform KPIs
- **Network Density**: Creator-fan interaction frequency
- **Discovery Success Rate**: % recommended creators gaining supporters
- **Retention Cohorts**: Day 1/7/30/90 retention tracking
- **Viral Coefficient**: New users per existing user (target 0.7-1.0)
- **Transaction Volume**: Total USDC flow through platform

### Web3-Specific KPIs
- **Wallet Adoption Rate**: % users upgrading to full Web3 features
- **NFT Engagement**: Minting, trading, utility usage rates
- **Token Holder Activity**: Governance participation, staking rates
- **Cross-Chain Usage**: Multi-blockchain transaction patterns

## Potencjalne ryzyka i mitygacja

### 1. Regulatory Risk w Web3 Space
**Ryzyko**: Changing crypto regulations, compliance requirements
**Mitygacja**:
- Proactive regulatory engagement w kluczowych jurysdykcjach

- Modular architecture pozwalająca na szybkie compliance changes
- Legal reserve fund 10-15% przychodów na regulatory costs

### 2. Creator Concentration Risk
**Ryzyko**: Platform dependency na small number of top creators
**Mitygacja**:
- Incentivize long-tail creator development
[Medium](https://medium.com/sequoia-capital/two-sided-marketplaces-and-engagement-ded7d
5dcfe71)
- Diversified creator acquisition across niches
- Anti-concentration policies (cap na single creator revenue %)

### 3. Network Effects Chicken-Egg Problem
**Ryzyko**: Need creators to attract fans, need fans to attract creators [MVP
Workshop](https://mvpworkshop.co/two-sided-marketplace/)
[Springer](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12525-020-00409-4)
**Mitygacja**:
- Celebrity/influencer acquisition w launch phase
- Paid creator acquisition program (guaranteed income for 3 months)
- Cross-platform creator migration incentives

### 4. Technical Scalability Challenges
**Ryzyko**: Blockchain transaction costs, speed limitations
**Mitygacja**:
- Layer 2 scaling solutions (Polygon, Arbitrum integration)
- Payment batching i aggregation systems
- Hybrid on/off-chain architecture dla high-frequency operations

### 5. User Experience Complexity
**Ryzyko**: Web3 complexity deterring mainstream adoption
**Mitygacja**:
- Progressive Web3 disclosure (start Web2-like)
- Extensive user education i support
- Alternative centralized backup systems dla edge cases

### 6. Competitive Response Risk
**Ryzyko**: Incumbent platforms copying Web3 features
**Mitygacja**:
- Speed to market advantage w Web3 features
- Patent defensible innovations where possible
- Strong creator lock-in through superior economics
- Community-owned governance jako competitive moat

### 7. Market Volatility Impact

**Ryzyko**: Crypto price volatility affecting user behavior
**Mitygacja**:
- Stablecoin focus (USDC) dla predictable value
- Fiat display options dla volatile crypto markets
- Hedging mechanisms dla creator income stability
